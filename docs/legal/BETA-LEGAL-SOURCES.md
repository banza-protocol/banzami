## OPEN LEGAL QUESTIONS FOR COUNSEL

1. **E-money / payments licensing.** A non-monetary Sandbox (test money, no
   settlement, no e-money issuance, no withdrawals, no real transfers) is
   intended to fall **outside** financial-institution / PSP / e-money licensing
   under the Angolan framework (BNA). **UNVERIFIED — for counsel.** The Terms
   therefore make **no** licensing claim and state plainly that Financial Live
   is unavailable and out of scope.
2. **Registration/notification duty to the APD.** Lei n.º 22/11 provides for
   controller obligations toward the authority. Whether Banzami must
   register/notify a processing operation for the Beta, and any prior
   authorisation for international transfers (hosting/subprocessors abroad),
   **must be confirmed by counsel** before Financial Live.
3. **International data transfers.** Subprocessors (Cloudflare, Resend, Firebase,
   IONOS) may process data outside Angola. The lawful-transfer basis under
   Lei n.º 22/11 (Cap. relating to transfers) **must be confirmed by counsel.**
   Documented factually in SUBPROCESSORS.md.
4. **Cookies / electronic communications.** No Angola-specific cookie/ePrivacy
   statute was identified as clearly applicable; the public site uses only
   strictly-necessary/functional cookies, so no consent banner is used. Confirm
   with counsel that this is adequate.
5. **Consumer / marketing communications consent.** If marketing emails are ever
   sent, a separate opt-in is required. None are sent today.
